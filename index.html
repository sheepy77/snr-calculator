SNR = Stake Not Returned — if the bonus bet wins, the bookmaker leg pays winnings only. If it loses, the bookmaker leg costs nothing, since it was never real money. Percentages are your return relative to the bonus bet stake. With the auto lay stake, "wins" and "loses" should show the same $ and % — that's the guarantee working. A manual lay stake (e.g. due to limited Betfair liquidity) can break that match, so both outcomes are shown separately.
